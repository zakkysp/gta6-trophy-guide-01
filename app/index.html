    About this guide

    Every trophy shows its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them (as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on all 55). Each guide is written in our own words and names its sources beside it — a specific step with no source gets no credit, so where a step could not be verified we say so plainly instead of inventing a location.

    Key facts

    • 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 are hidden on PlayStation until earned.
    • No online trophies — the whole list is single-player and offline.
    • Estimated difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours to the platinum (PowerPyx).
    • One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free-roam continues after the story.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off during a trophy run (PowerPyx, GTABase).
    • Keep 15 manual save slots in mind: save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the rest for cleanup.

    Sources

      Honesty notes

      • Four trophies were still unconfirmed when this guide was written on launch day (19 November 2026): In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. PowerPyx lists them as unknown, with a best guess only. Their guides say so — they do not invent a solution.
      • Whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation counts toward Positive Mental Attitude is unconfirmed (PowerPyx). Do it anyway — it costs a minute and it is the only step that can disappear.
      • Counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 WAiNK scans and the rest) come from the official descriptions and PSNProfiles' tracked targets.
